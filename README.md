# Turn Signal

A chase-cam driving game. Take every corner inside the box, dodge what's in the road, grab coins, and climb the leaderboard. Every 4 turns takes you somewhere new, and at turn 16 your ride takes off.

**Play:** https://zubin7-ux.github.io/turn-signal/

- `game.html` is the source (it also runs as a Claude artifact).
- `online.js` is the public site's leaderboard and live ghost cars (Supabase).
- `python3 build.py` builds `index.html` from the two.
