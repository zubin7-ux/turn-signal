    Turn Signal

    The road bends. You bend with it. Every clean turn makes you faster, and every 4 turns takes you somewhere new. Make it to turn 16 and your ride takes off.

    Everyone drives the same road today. Other players show up as ghost cars, and the best runs go on the leaderboard.

    • ←→Change lanes to dodge whatever is in the road, holes included. Steer off the edge and your run is over.
    • ←→Turn only once you are inside the corner box. Too early or too late and you crash. Turning early in the box puts you in the near lane, late in the far lane.
    • A red target hunts for you. When it locks on, get out of that lane before the torpedo lands.
