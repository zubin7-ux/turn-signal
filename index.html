    HOW TO PLAY

    Turn in the box, dodge everything, keep going. You never stop speeding up, and the road keeps getting meaner. New world every 4 turns. At turn 16 you take off into the sky, then space, then touch down for another lap of worlds.

    Every run is new: a random starting zone, a shuffled route, and often a twist like Rush Hour or Fog Bank.

    • ←→TAP L/RChange lanes to dodge whatever is in the road, holes included. Steer off the edge and your run is over.
    • ←→TAP L/RTurn only once you are inside the corner box. Too early or too late and you crash. Turning early in the box puts you in the near lane, late in the far lane.
    • A red target hunts for you. When it locks on, get out of that lane before the torpedo lands.
    • ?Drive through a ? box for an item: shield, turbo, magnet or jump. Press Space or ↑ (or tap the item button) to use it.Tap the item button in the bottom corner to use it.
    • Grab coins, then spend them on mystery boxes to find new rides.
